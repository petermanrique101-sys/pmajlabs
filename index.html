About P MAJ LABS

P MAJ LABS LLC is a Texas software studio based in McKinney, founded by Pedro Manrique. Before writing software for other businesses, Pedro spent four years running one: a Nutrishop retail location opened in 2022, with everything that comes with it.

That is the difference in practice. We have been the customer for bad software. We have paid for the website that nobody could edit, and used the point of sale that did not talk to anything else. So we build the opposite: tools that match how the business actually operates, from someone who has done payroll on a Sunday night.

ScanProgress came directly out of that. Members were getting InBody scans and walking out with a paper printout they would lose in a week. So we built the app, submitted it to both stores, and it now runs in a live retail location.
